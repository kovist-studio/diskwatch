# Trademark policy

The **DiskWatch** name and logo are trademarks of the DiskWatch project
(Taeyun Kim). They are **not** covered by the MIT license that governs the
source code.

## What this means

- **The code is MIT.** You may use, modify, and redistribute the source under
  the terms in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
- **The name and logo are not.** The MIT grant covers code, not branding.

## If you fork or redistribute

You are welcome to fork the code. If you distribute a modified build, you must
**rebrand** it:

- Don't call it "DiskWatch" or use a confusingly similar name.
- Don't use the DiskWatch logo or icon.
- Don't imply your build is the official DiskWatch or is endorsed by it.

Unmodified builds distributed through the official channels (see
["Official downloads"](README.md#official-downloads) in the README) may use the
name and logo.

## Why

DiskWatch inspects the filesystem and, later, removes files and checks links.
Users need to trust that a program called "DiskWatch" is the real thing. The
trademark keeps the name pointing at official, checksum-verified builds, so a
malicious repackage can't ride on the name. See [DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md)
for the full reasoning behind the MIT + trademark posture.
